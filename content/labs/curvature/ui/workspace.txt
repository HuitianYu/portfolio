            <h1>
              Explore crystal shape.
              <br />
              <em>Reveal its curvature.</em>
            </h1>
            <p>
              Inspect simulated crystal surfaces, compare intact, agglomerated and broken
              geometries, and calculate local curvature directly on the triangular mesh.

          <article>
            <div class="eyebrow">Crystal shape</div>
            <h2>
              A closer look at
              <br />
              crystal morphology.
            </h2>
            <p>
              Select a crystal by its original size group and Rheinhold roundness. Render the
              original mesh, then colour the surface by mean, Gaussian or principal curvature.
            </p>
            <div class="step">
              <b>01</b>
              <div>
                <h3>Choose a crystal</h3>
                <p>
                  Three simulated size groups, with single, agglomerate and broken meshes for every
                  crystal.
                </p>
              </div>
            </div>
            <div class="step">
              <b>02</b>
              <div>
                <h3>Inspect the mesh</h3>
                <p>Rotate, zoom, show mesh edges and animate the camera.</p>
              </div>
            </div>
            <div class="step">
              <b>03</b>
              <div>
                <h3>Calculate curvature</h3>
                <p>
                  Mixed Voronoi vertex areas, angle defects and the cotangent Laplacian describe
                  local surface geometry.
                </p>
              </div>
            </div>
          </article>
          <figure>
            <img
              alt="Glycine crystal with surface curvature colouring"

            />
            <figcaption>Glycine surface curvature · supplied reference image</figcaption>
          </figure>
        </div>
        <div class="note">
          Size groups refer to the half-diagonal length of the unabraded crystal. A filename ending
          in 0p063543 encodes Rheinhold roundness 0.063543, obtained from the Minkowski-addition
          reference calculation.
        </div>
